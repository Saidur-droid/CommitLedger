# Season 4 Validation Sprint

Goal: collect a small amount of real problem validation without delaying the build.

## Target respondents

Prioritize 3–5 people who actually operate one of:
- open-source repositories;
- contributor/bounty programs;
- foundation grants;
- ecosystem developer programs;
- external engineering contributor workflows.

## Three questions

1. Where does the process between “PR merged” and “contributor paid/settled” break or become manual today?
2. Would independent evidence + role-separated approval + a replay-safe receipt solve a real problem for your program? Why or why not?
3. What would you need to see before piloting this on one repository?

Optional:
- Who owns the budget?
- What tool/process do you use now?
- What would make this a “must have” rather than “nice to have”?

## Evidence rules

Record:
- date;
- respondent role/company/community only if they consent;
- exact or faithful short summary;
- strongest objection;
- pilot condition.

Do not manufacture testimonials. Do not convert polite interest into a customer claim.

## Submission use

Good:
> “Three maintainers we spoke with consistently described manual approval/audit handoffs after merge.”

Only say this after three real conversations actually support it.

Bad:
> “Teams love CommitLedger.”

Never use unsupported generalizations.
