Verified from official rules:

- Contest starts **14 Sep 2026, 6:00 AM PT**.
- Contest ends **12 Oct 2026, 11:59 PM PT**.
- Bangladesh equivalent: **13 Oct 2026, 12:59**.

- Entrant must be age of majority in country/residence or at least 18, whichever is older.
